# Stage 2 — 3CC-Coupled Static Optimization: the Fatigue Label

Generated by `Code/run_fatigue_so.py`. This is the scientific core of the fatigue
labels — it adds the capacity-decline mechanism that plain ID+SO lacked.

## Why this step exists
Plain ID+SO gave **flat** activation across reps (mean flexor 0.435→0.450), because
Stage-1 kinematic fatigue *reduces* demand. Real fatigue holds demand while
**capacity falls**, so activation **rises** (Potvin & Bent 2010: aEMG up, MPF
−25..29 %). The three-compartment controller (3CC) supplies that mechanism.

## Method (post-hoc 3CC + SO coupling — Related-Work §2.2.8)
1. Baseline SO (from `stage2_labels.csv`): per-flexor force `F0`, activation `a0`,
   and ID elbow moment `M(t)`.
2. Per-frame force capacity inferred from SO: `Cap0 = F0/a0` (= Fmax·fL·fV).
3. **3CC per flexor** (Xia & Frey-Law 2008), **elbow** rates from Frey-Law et al.
   (2012): **F = 0.00912 /s, R = 0.00094 /s**.
   `dMF/dt = F·MA − R·MF` (MA, MF in % of motor units); `capacity = 1 − MF/100`.
4. **Re-solve elbow load-sharing each frame** with the fatigued bound:
   `min Σ(F_m/Cap0_m)²  s.t.  Σ r_m(θ)·F_m = M(t),  0 ≤ F_m ≤ capacity_m·Cap0_m`.
   The active fraction `MA = 100·(F_m/Cap0_m)` then drives the next `MF` step —
   positive feedback (more fatigue → more activation → more fatigue). If the
   flexors' summed capacity < demand → task-failure (saturation) is flagged.

Moment arms `r_m(θ)` are the validated analytic virtual-work values; the small
per-frame QP is solved with SciPy (run in the `biomech` env).

## Result (10-rep / 41 s bout)
```
rep   mean a_fresh   mean a_fatigued   rise    MF(BIClong)
 1       0.435          0.455         +4.5%      0.3%
 5       0.443          0.465         +5.0%      2.5%
10       0.450          0.471         +4.6%      5.7%
task-failure frames: 0
```
- **Capacity declines monotonically** (BIClong fatigued motor units 0.3 % → 5.7 %).
- **Fatigued activation is consistently above fresh** (+4.5–5.3 % within each rep)
  and drifts up **+3.5 %** rep1→rep10 — now the **correct direction** (vs the flat
  ID+SO result). No task failure at this sub-maximal 2 kg / short bout.

## Interpretation & honest scope
- The effect is **modest at 10 reps** because it's a short, sub-maximal bout — only
  ~6 % capacity loss. That is consistent with the elbow F/R magnitudes (slow
  recovery, moderate fatigue rate).
- **It scales with the protocol.** Potvin & Bent's 25–29 % decline is reached over
  many more repetitions; running the **30–100-rep** production motion (Stage-1
  `N_REPS`) will accumulate `MF` much further and produce the pronounced
  rising-activation signature. The 10-rep run proves the mechanism is correct and
  monotonic; rep count sets the magnitude.
- Two complementary fatigue channels now exist for the AI stage:
  - **observable (vision):** slowing / reduced ROM / tremor (Stage-1 kinematics),
  - **internal (label):** rising activation + declining capacity (this file).

## Output — the fatigue-correct AI labels
`Results/Stage2/stage2_fatigue_labels.csv`, per frame:
`time, rep, fatigue_level, elbow_moment, task_failure`, and for each flexor
(BIClong, BICshort, BRA, BRD_hand): `a0_` (fresh SO), `afat_` (3CC-coupled),
`MF_` (% fatigued), `cap_` (remaining capacity).
